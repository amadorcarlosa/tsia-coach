Phase outline · 4 steps total

Every walkthrough runs the same four phases.

You act at each one. The scaffold holds the parts you are not working on yet, so the only thing in front of you is the step you are actually doing.

1
Name the unknown

Say what the letter stands for, in words, before touching any symbols.

2
Translate the phrases

Map each piece of English to a fragment of algebra. One phrase at a time.

3
Assemble

Snap the fragments together into the expression the question is asking for.

4
Check against the choices

Find your expression among A–D, and see why the other three are there.
